BudgetPlanner is the household ledger we actually open on a phone. Recurring bills, income, and credits live in one plan, and you invite people by email with view or edit. This-month totals only count what is due in the current calendar month, which is the number we argue about at the table.

English is the default. German is ready when we need it.

Live: [budget.darkavianlabs.com](https://budget.darkavianlabs.com)

## Gotchas

- App and session SQLite files must be different paths.
- The owner cannot leave a plan. They delete it.
